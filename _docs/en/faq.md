## Accounts

### How do I sign up?

With an email and password, or with GitHub or Bitbucket, at [gitpay.me](https://gitpay.me). If you'll work on bounties, sign up with GitHub: bounties can only be paid to accounts signed in with GitHub.

### What are roles?

Provider, Contributor, Funding (sponsor) and Maintainer. They change what your dashboard shows; they don't restrict what you can do. Change them in **Profile → User account → Roles**. See [What is Gitpay?](/docs/en/getting-started/).

### Can I use Gitpay in Portuguese?

Yes. Gitpay and its documentation are available in English and Portuguese.

### How do I stop email notifications?

In **Preferences**, clear **I want to receive relevant notifications from Gitpay** and select **Save settings**.

## Fees

### How much does Gitpay charge?

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Payment requests (new) | 5% of the amount charged, plus the processing fee |
| Payment requests (older links) | 8% of the amount after the processing fee |
| Bounties | 8% added for the sponsor (no fee from $5,000; 18% for private issues), and the contributor receives 92% |
| Payouts | No Gitpay fee. Whop may charge a withdrawal fee. |

Details: [How payments and fees work](/docs/en/payments/).

### Why did my processing fee change between two payments?

Whop's processing fee depends on how the customer paid and where they are, for example card vs. bank transfer, or a domestic vs. an international card. Each payment's breakdown shows the exact fee.

## Payments

### Does my customer need a Gitpay account?

No. They open the link and pay on the checkout.

### Which currency can I charge in?

Payment requests are priced in USD. You're paid out in the currency of the country you chose when setting up payouts.

### Can I change the amount of a payment request?

No. Create a new one and deactivate the old one. You can edit the title, description and settings. See [Request payment from a customer](/docs/en/service-provider/).

## Payouts

### Why can't I create a payment request?

Your payout account isn't active yet. Finish verification and add a payout method on Whop. See [Set up payouts](/docs/en/whop-payout-setup/).

### Why is my balance pending?

Card payments take 1–4 days to settle before they can be paid out. Only the **available** balance can be withdrawn.

### Which countries are supported?

Whop pays out to more than 100 countries. See the [supported countries](https://gitpay.me/#/countries) page.
