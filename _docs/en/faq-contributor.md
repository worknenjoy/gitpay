### Do I need to be assigned to get paid?

No. The bounty is paid to the author of the merged pull request that solves the issue. Applying and getting assigned helps you avoid duplicate work, but it isn't required. See [Work on a funded issue](/docs/en/contributor/).

### Two of us solved the same issue. Who gets paid?

The author of the pull request that was merged. Only one pull request closes the issue, so check **Solutions sent** on the issue page before you start, and tell the owner you're working on it.

### Why don't I get the full bounty?

Gitpay keeps 8% of the bounty when it's paid out, so you receive 92%. A $100 bounty pays you $92.

### My pull request was merged but the check fails. Why?

The usual causes:

- the pull request was merged into a fork, not the original repository;
- the description doesn't reference the issue (add `Fixes #123`);
- the issue is still open on GitHub;
- you're signed in to Gitpay with a different GitHub account, or with email instead of GitHub.

See [Submit a solution](/docs/en/pull-requests/).

### When can I withdraw the money?

Once the bounty is transferred to your Whop balance and becomes available. Then go to **My Payouts → Request payout**. See [Payouts](/docs/en/payouts/).

### The issue has no bounty. Can I still get paid?

Yes, in two ways:

- send your solution and **request payment from the maintainer**; or
- **make an offer** proposing a bounty before you start. See [Work on a funded issue](/docs/en/contributor/).

You can also [import your merged pull request](/docs/en/delivering-work/) as a payment request and post the payment link on the pull request.

### What does "To pay later" mean on a bounty?

The sponsor pledged that amount but hasn't paid it yet. They're asked to pay when a solution is accepted. Money marked **Paid in advance** is already held by Gitpay.

### I forgot to submit my solution.

Submit it now. Gitpay reminds pull request authors on the 26th of each month. After two reminders, the bounty is refunded to the sponsors.
