## Do I need to be assigned before I start?
Usually yes. If you start work before approval, you risk duplicating effort or working against a different expected scope.

## What counts as “done”?
Typically: your Pull Request matches the task requirements, passes automated checks (if any), and is accepted by the maintainer.

## When do I get paid?
Payment is released after acceptance according to the project’s workflow. See [payments](/docs/en/payments).

## What if I get stuck?
Share what you tried, what you observed, and ask specific questions on the issue/PR.

## Can I be paid for a contribution that did not start as a bounty?
Potentially, if the project agrees to pay for the accepted work and establishes a payment route. Do not assume that a merged Pull Request creates an automatic entitlement. Ask the maintainer to confirm scope, amount, and recipient. See [What is Gitpay?](/docs/en/getting-started/).

## Why is my transfer not in my bank account?
A credited transfer and a payout are different stages. Check [Claims](/docs/en/claims/), your payout settings, and [How payouts work](/docs/en/payouts/).

## Can I use a Payment Request for direct client work?
Yes. If you delivered a service directly to a customer, use [Charge customers](/docs/en/service-provider/) for the separate Payment Request path.
